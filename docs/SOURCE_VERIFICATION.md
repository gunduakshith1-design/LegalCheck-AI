## 3. RULE 6 — MANDATORY DECLARATIONS (The Core Rule)

This is the **primary rule** for image-based compliance. Every sub-rule in Rule 6(1) defines a field that MUST appear on the package.

### Rule 6(1)(a) — Manufacturer/Packer/Importer Name & Address
> **Source wording:** *"the name and address of the manufacturer, or where the manufacturer is not the packer, the name and address of the manufacturer and packer and for any imported package the name and address of the importer shall be mentioned"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **Key details:** Must include "Manufactured by" / "Packed by" / "Imported by" qualifier
- **Exception:** Food articles follow Prevention of Food Adulteration Act instead
- **PDF pages:** 5-6 (Rule 6(1)(a), Rule 10)

### Rule 6(1)(b) — Common/Generic Name
> **Source wording:** *"The common or generic names of the commodity contained in the package and in case of packages with more than one product, the name and number or quantity of each product shall be mentioned"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **Key details:** Must be generic (not just brand name)
- **PDF page:** 5

### Rule 6(1)(c) — Net Quantity
> **Source wording:** *"The net quantity, in terms of the standard unit of weight or measure, of the commodity contained in the package"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **Key details:** Must use SI units; no misleading qualifiers ("minimum", "about")
- **PDF pages:** 5, 11-13 (Rules 11-13 for unit specifications)

### Rule 6(1)(d) — Month and Year of Manufacture/Packing/Import
> **Source wording:** *"The month and year in which the commodity is manufactured or pre-packed or imported shall be mentioned"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes (with exceptions)
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **Exceptions:** Bidis, incense sticks, LPG cylinders, food (follow PFA), cosmetics (follow Drugs & Cosmetics Rules), seeds (follow Seeds Act)
- **PDF pages:** 5-6

### Rule 6(1)(e) — Retail Sale Price (MRP)
> **Source wording:** *"the retail sale price of the package"*

Definition in Rule 2(m):
> *"retail sale price means the maximum price at which the commodity in packaged form may be sold to the consumer and the price shall be printed on the package in the manner given below: 'Maximum or Max. retail price Rs/ .......inclusive of all taxes' or 'MRP Rs/ .........incl., of all taxes'"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes (with exceptions)
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **Key details:** Must say "inclusive of all taxes"; specific format allowed
- **Exceptions:** Bidis; LPG cylinders under Administrative Price Mechanism; alcoholic beverages follow state excise laws
- **PDF pages:** 3, 7

### Rule 6(2) — Consumer Care Details
> **Source wording:** *"Every package shall bear the name, address, telephone number, e mail address, if available, of the person who can be or the office which can be, contacted, in case of consumer complaints"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **Key details:** Phone number mandatory; email "if available"
- **PDF page:** 7

### Rule 6(3) — No Individual Stickers for Alteration
> **Source wording:** *"It shall not be permissible to affix individual stickers on the package for altering or making declaration required under these rules"*
- Exception: Sticker for reducing MRP (lower price) is permitted, must not cover original MRP

- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Visually detectable:** PARTIALLY — can detect if sticker is covering declarations

### Rule 9(4) — Language Requirement
> **Source wording:** *"The particulars of the declarations required to be specified under this rule on a package shall either be in Hindi in Devnagri script or in English"*

- **Mandatory:** Yes
- **Visually detectable:** YES
- **PDF page:** 10

## 5. CONDITIONAL DEPENDENCIES

These requirements change based on product context:

| Condition | Affected Rules | Impact |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **Product is food** | 6(1)(a) Expl. III, 6(1)(d) | PFA/FSSAI provisions apply instead of LMPC |
| **Product is cosmetic** | 6(1)(d) | Drugs & Cosmetics Rules apply for date |
| **Product is alcoholic** | 6(1)(e) | State excise laws may override MRP requirement |
| **Product is a drug (scheduled/non-scheduled)** | 26(c) | Entirely exempt from LMPC rules |
| **Product is imported** | 6(1)(a) | Must show importer name & address |
| **Product is imported + manufactured outside India, packed in India** | 10(1) proviso | Must show packer/importer address on principal display panel |
| **Product is agricultural (>50kg)** | 26(d) | Exempt from rules |
| **Product is fast food from restaurant/hotel** | 26(b) | Exempt from rules |
| **Net ≤ 10g or 10ml** | 26(a) | Entirely exempt (note: 10-20g/ml only needs MRP + net qty) |
| **Net quantity by number** | 13(5)(ii) | Use symbol N or U |
| **Dimensions relevant to price** | 6(1)(f), 14, 15 | Additional dimension declarations needed |
| **Package is returnable (soft drinks)** | 8(2) | MRP may be on crown cap or bottle |
| **Package capacity ≤ 5 cubic cm** | 7(1), 10(1) | Reduced requirements |

## 7. IMPORTANT EXCEPTIONS (Potential False Results)

### Critical Exceptions
1. **Food products** (Rule 6(1)(a) Expl. III): PFA/FSSAI provisions apply — manufacturer address, date, and quantity declarations follow different rules. Our system may flag non-compliance when the product actually follows food-specific regulations.

2. **Cosmetics** (Rule 6(1)(d) proviso): Date declarations follow Drugs & Cosmetics Rules, 1945 — not LMPC.

3. **Drugs** (Rule 26(c)): Scheduled/non-scheduled formulations under DPCO are entirely exempt from LMPC Rules.

4. **Alcoholic beverages** (Rule 6(1)(e) proviso): MRP follows state excise laws — may be absent or differently formatted.

5. **Packages ≤ 10g/10ml** (Rule 26(a)): Entirely exempt. Packages between 10-20g/ml need only MRP and net quantity.

6. **Industrial/institutional consumers** (Rule 3(b)): Exempt from Chapter II provisions.

7. **Quantities > 25kg/25L** (Rule 3(a)): Exempt (except cement/fertilizer up to 50kg).

8. **Imported goods manufactured abroad, packed in India** (Rule 10(1) proviso): Must show both importer AND packer address.

9. **Brand owner as marketer** (Rule 6(1)(a) Expl. II): Brand owner held responsible even if different from manufacturer.

### Detection False-Positive Risks
- OCR may miss text on curved/reflective surfaces
- Partially visible labels may appear as missing declarations
- Multi-language labels may confuse language detection
- Regulatory text (FSSAI, BIS marks) may appear as missing manufacturer info
- Sticker-reduced MRP may be flagged as alteration violation
